# Channel map — hearing a signal is not transmitting it

The station has a local cue channel and an outgoing programme channel. The presenter hears the cue channel in the booth headphones. The public programme feed is supplied by the playout queue and the programme mixer.

A live microphone or audible cue signal does not prove the programme channel is transmitting the scheduled master. During silent hold, the cue channel may continue to carry the presenter's microphone while the programme channel sends silence.

The programme mixer recorded silent hold from 23:17:01 until the verified vault path was loaded at 23:21:10 UTC. The local cue channel remained active throughout that interval. This explains why Mara heard audio while the public feed had no programme material.

The channel map rules out treating booth headphone audio as proof that Q-221 found its file. It does not establish why the preservation operation occurred, and it does not describe the state of either door.
