# The Last Broadcast — case brief

Station WREN prepared a one-off midnight programme called The Glasshouse Session. At 23:17 UTC on 14 November, the outgoing programme feed went silent. The studio microphone was still live locally, but no master recording played on the programme channel.

The control room was locked. At the production desk, the scheduled master file could no longer be found at its usual path. A presenter reported seeing an engineer near the room shortly before the silence. A producer said the session had already been preserved in the archive.

The archive contains operational records, interviews, a clock check, and a file inventory. Each source has its own scope: an access reader describes a particular door, a witness describes what they believed they saw, and a software log identifies an account rather than the person holding its credentials.

Your task is to establish what happened, which explanations the records contradict, and what remains unresolved. Do not treat a missing production file as proof that its content was destroyed. The material does not include surveillance footage, a voice recording of the operator, or a completed forensic examination of the production console.
