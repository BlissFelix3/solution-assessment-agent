# Maintenance notice — fallback policy

The station's ordinary fallback is a licensed loop loaded by the programme operator. It is not a search of the master archive. During this one-off session, automatic fallback was disabled under maintenance change M-62 at 22:50 UTC.

With M-62 active, SOURCE_NOT_FOUND triggers silent hold until an operator supplies a valid path. The operator can still load a verified recording manually. The archive inventory records the later path correction and restored feed.

The maintenance notice was approved before the preservation request. Its timestamp does not show that the duty engineer issued R-409 or caused the production path to move. The notice explains why the system stayed silent rather than switching to a loop.

No document in this collection records a financial motive, a private message planning sabotage, or the identity of the physical console operator. Those questions remain unresolved by this archive, even when the operational sequence can be established.
